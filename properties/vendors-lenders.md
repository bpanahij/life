## 💰 Lenders — Construction Loans

One closing, converts to mortgage when build is done.

| Lender | Type | Why | Contact |

| **Austin Capital Mortgage** | Mortgage broker | Access to 100+ lenders. Fast closings (7–21 days). | https://www.austincapitalmortgage.com/construction-loan |
| **TexasBank** | Texas bank | Full-service construction-to-permanent loans. | https://www.texasbank.com/mortgage/construction-loans |
| **MortgageATX** | Austin local | No requalifying at modification. 9-month construction term. | https://mortgageatx.com/construction-loans/ |

### Option B: HELOC Expansion (SF Property for Sonoma)
**Start with your current HELOC lender** — they already have your property on file and can tell you how much more you can borrow.

### Current Construction Loan Rates (June 2026)
| Product | Rate Range |
|---------|-----------|
| Construction-to-permanent (prime) | **6.5%–7.5%** |
| Construction-only (variable) | **7%–9%** |
| HELOC (national avg) | **7.43%** |
| HELOC (Texas, good credit) | **6.75%–8.5%** |

### What Lenders Will Want
- Credit score: 780 ✅ (yours is excellent)
- Income: $650K ✅
- Builder contract & plans: Need from builder
- Construction budget & schedule: Need from builder
- Down payment: Typically 20–30% (but your existing equity in the lot counts)
