## 2. Austin Property — Construction Loan (best option)

**HELOC?** ❌ Not available — Texas law (Section 50(a)(6)) limits HELOCs to **primary owner-occupied residences only**. Since you live in SF, the Austin duplex doesn't qualify.

**Construction-to-Permanent Loan:** ✅ This is your best path.

### How it works
| Phase | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Construction phase** | Lender funds draws as work progresses (typically 5–6 draws). You pay interest-only during construction. |
| **Completion** | Loan converts to a permanent mortgage (15–30 yr fixed or ARM). |
| **One closing** | Single closing — you lock the rate at the start or at conversion. |

### Can you get $1M with a 780 credit score?

| Factor | Your Situation | Verdict |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Credit score | 780 / 800+ combined | ✅ Excellent |
| Income | ~$650K–$700K | ✅ Strong |
| DTI (debt-to-income) | Even with $1M loan, DTI would be ~35–40% | ✅ Manageable |
| Down payment / equity | Existing property equity (~$350K) counts | ✅ Substantial equity |
| **Likely approval** | | **✅ Very likely** |

With your income, credit, and existing equity in the Austin property itself, a $1M construction loan should be **very achievable**.
