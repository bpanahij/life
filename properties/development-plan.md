## 🏘️ Austin: Tear-Down & Rebuild

### The Vision
- Tear down existing 1950s duplex (1,161 sq ft)
- Build a modern 2–3 story home
  - Main building: 3 stories with roof deck
  - Second unit: workspace / home office that doubles as apartment
  - Pool
  - Nice driveway, front fence, landscaping
- **Total budget: ~$1,000,000**

### What $1M Builds in Austin
| Construction Type | Cost/sq ft | Typical Size for $1M |
|------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Semi-custom | $225–$325/sq ft | 3,000–4,400 sq ft |
| Full custom | $325–$500/sq ft | 2,000–3,000 sq ft |
| Luxury custom | $500–$1,000+ sq ft | 1,000–2,000 sq ft |

At luxury custom level ($500/sq ft), $1M builds ~2,000 sq ft. At semi-custom, you could get 3,500+ sq ft.

### Estimated Post-Build Value
**Oakmont Heights** median home value: **$881,690**

A *brand new* modern 2–3 story home with pool, roof deck, and guest unit in Oakmont Heights would be at the **top of the market**. Estimated finished value:

| Scenario | Est. Value | Gain/(Loss) |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Conservative (2,000 sq ft semi-custom) | $1.2M–$1.4M | $200K–$400K gain |
| Moderate (2,500 sq ft custom) | $1.4M–$1.7M | $400K–$700K gain |
| Best case (3,000+ sq ft luxury) | $1.7M–$2.2M | $700K–$1.2M gain |

> A 7,087 sq ft lot in this location with a brand new modern build would be extremely rare and command premium pricing.

### Financing Options

| Option | How It Works | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Construction-to-Permanent Loan** | Single loan that covers construction, then converts to mortgage | $1M project, one closing |
| **Builder Line of Credit** | Credit line for construction draws, separate from mortgage | Flexibility, staged draws |
| **Cash-Out Refinance** | Refi existing mortgage to pull equity + construction funds | If existing rate is high |
| **HELOC** ❌ | **Not available** — Texas law (80% CLTV) only allows HELOCs on **primary owner-occupied residences** | N/A for this property |
