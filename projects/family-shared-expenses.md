# 🏡 Family Shared Expenses Plan

**Status:** Draft
**Last updated:** June 14, 2026

---

## The Goal

Create a single central account structure where **all community/household/family expenses** flow through one shared credit card. Anything charged to this card is a **mutual responsibility** — not Brian's or Barbara's individually. It's a family expense, paid from family funds.

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **Shared is shared.** If it goes on the family card, it's a joint obligation — no splitting, no "whose turn," no tracking who paid last.
2. **Personal stays personal.** Individual discretionary spending stays on individual cards.
3. **Simple, not perfect.** The goal is less friction, not perfect categorization. Accept rough consensus over granular tracking.
4. **Transparent by default.** Both Brian and Barbara see the statement monthly. No surprises.
5. **Automatic where possible.** Recurring family bills (utilities, subscriptions, kid stuff) auto-charge to the family card.

## Account & Card Structure

### Option A (Recommended): Joint Credit Card + Joint Checking

```
Joint Checking Account
├── Both incomes deposit here (or fixed transfer from individual accounts)
├── Auto-pay for the family credit card
└── Both have debit cards for cash needs

Joint Credit Card
├── Primary cardholder: whoever has better credit / rewards
├── Authorized user: the other
├── All family expenses charged here
├── Rewards (cash back / points) → family fund
└── Auto-paid in full from joint checking each month
```

### Option B: One Card, Split Deposit

```
Keep individual checking accounts
├── Each sets up automatic transfer to a joint account
│   ├── Brian: $X per paycheck
│   └── Barbara: $Y per paycheck
│
Joint Account
└── Auto-pay the family card
```

### Option C: Add Authorized User on an Existing Card

```
Simplest start:
├── Add Barbara as AU on Brian's best rewards card (or vice versa)
├── Both use that card for family expenses
├── Track family charges separately (easier if used only for family)
└── Pay from whichever account you both agree on
```

## The "Family Fund" — How the Bill Gets Paid

### Monthly Flow

```
1. Family charges accumulate on the shared card
2. Statement comes due (typically 25-day grace period)
3. Payment drawn from the family checking account
4. Family checking funded by both incomes
```

### Funding Methods

**Recommended: Percentage of Income**

| Income Source | Amount | Contribution |
|--------------|--------|-------------|
| Brian (Tavus) | ~$25,000/mo gross | ~$17K net — contributes X% |
| Barbara (Salesforce) | ~$30K–$33K/mo gross | ~$21K net — contributes X% |
| **Total household** | **~$38K/mo net** | **Joint account gets $Y/mo** |

Family account gets funded by both parties at an agreed ratio (50/50, proportional to income, or a flat amount). The card gets paid in full every month — no carrying a balance.

### Suggested Contribution Method

Simplest: **50/50 flat contribution** to the joint account.

1. Calculate average monthly family expenses (estimate $8K–$12K/mo)
2. Each contributes half + a buffer (say $7K each → $14K in joint account)
3. If the balance grows, adjust contributions down. If it shrinks, adjust up.
4. Excess builds a small family reserve / vacation fund.

## Implementation Steps

### Phase 1: Pick the Card (Week 1)
| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Compare current cards for best rewards (travel/cash back/grocery bonus) | Brian |
| Decide who has better credit/rewards for primary holder | Both |
| Add the other as authorized user | Primary holder |

### Phase 2: Open Joint Account (Week 1–2)
| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Open joint checking at an existing bank (or a high-yield account) | Both |
| Set up direct deposit or recurring transfers from each income | Both |
| Link the family card for auto-pay | Both |

### Phase 3: Migrate Expenses (Weeks 2–4)
| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Move utilities, subscriptions, and recurring bills to the family card | Brian |
| Start using the family card for all grocery/household purchases | Both |
| Inform sitters, daycare, school of new payment card | Both |

### Phase 4: Review & Adjust (Month 2+)
| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| Review statement together | Monthly |
| Adjust contribution amounts if needed | Quarterly |
| Evaluate if any categories should move on/off the card | Quarterly |

## Gotchas & Guardrails

### What If…
| Scenario | Plan |
|----------|------|
| One person overspends on family card | It's a shared card — both see charges. The rule is: if in doubt, ask. Large purchases ($500+) should get a quick heads-up. |
| Disagreement on what's "family" | Default to "if it benefits the household or kids, it's family." If still unclear, leave it off the card and pay personally. |
| Emergency / unexpected expense | Put it on the family card. That's what the buffer is for. Discuss after. |
| Rewards / points | All rewards from the family card → family fund (vacations, upgrades). Not personal. |
| One person's income changes | Adjust contributions at the quarterly review. |

### Recommended Guardrails
- **No balance carried.** Pay in full every month. This card is a convenience, not a loan.
- **$500+ requires a text.** Quick heads-up before big charges.
- **Monthly 10-minute review.** Scan the statement together. Catch surprises early.
- **Annual true-up.** In January, reconcile contributions vs. actual spending and adjust.
